Patient Records Report

Generates a complete list of all registered patients including demographics, contact information, and registration details.

Report Contents:
Patient name, age, sex, civil status, address, contact number, PhilHealth number, occupation, and date of registration. Sorted alphabetically by last name.
📈
Disease Trends Report

Analyzes disease cases recorded within the selected year to identify patterns and trends in the community.

Note: This report is based on disease diagnoses and medical records entered by authorized health workers into the system. The data is analyzed to identify disease patterns and trends within the selected period. Results should be interpreted by qualified health personnel.
💉
Immunization Report

Provides a complete overview of vaccines administered to patients to monitor immunization coverage and activities.

Note: This report is based on vaccination and immunization records entered into the system by authorized health workers. It helps monitor immunization activities, track dose schedules, and identify patients with upcoming or missed vaccinations.
🤖
AI Data-Driven Insights

Uses Google Gemini 2.5 to analyze health data and generate AI-powered insights to support decision-making.

Important: The AI Data-Driven Decision-Making feature uses Google Gemini 2.5 to analyze relevant system data and generate data-based insights. The AI-generated results are intended as decision-support information only and should not replace the professional judgment of qualified healthcare personnel.
